Pratiksha Pandit

Geologist & Choreographer

My name is Pratiksha Pandit. I'm a Geologist & Choreographer. My education has provided me with a variety of information systems knowledge and practical skills; primarily in the field of geology. I have Diploma Degrees in Kathak & Bharatnatyam.

My work experience as a Dance Instructor at Kendriya Vidhyalaya, Vadodara, India provided me with good command over teaching dance to students of different age groups from beginner level to expert level. I have experience in teaching dance forms like Kathak, Bharatnatyam, Bollywood and Freestyle.
